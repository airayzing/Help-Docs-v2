{% hint style="info" %}

{% endhint %}

#### Departments

The Departments section allows you to create and manage departments based on your organizational needs. You can assign specific team members to each department to streamline operations and improve efficiency.

**How to Create a Department:**

1. Navigate to the **Departments** section.
2. Enter the department name in the **Name** field.
3. Add a short description for the department, if necessary.
4. Click on the **Add Department** button to save the department.
