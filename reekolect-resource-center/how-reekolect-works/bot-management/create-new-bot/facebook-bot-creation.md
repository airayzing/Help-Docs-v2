# 😀 Facebook Bot Creation

### How to Create Your Facebook Chatbot? <a href="#how-to-create-your-facebook-chatbot" id="how-to-create-your-facebook-chatbot"></a>

Follow these quick steps to create your Facebook chatbot:-

1. To start creating your new chatbot, click **"Create New Bot."**
2. Select the primary purpose for creating your bot and proceed further.
3. Select "**Facebook Chatbot**" to begin creating your chatbot.
4. Now click on login and connect your facebook account.

<figure><img src="../../../../.gitbook/assets/image (3).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

5. Enter your phone number/email address and click “**Log in**”.
6. If you want to create a new Facebook account, click on “**Create New Account**”

<figure><img src="../../../../.gitbook/assets/image (4).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

7. Then select your business account which you want Reekolect to access and use chatbot for. You can opt for all the accounts or select a specific business account only.
8. Then click on "**Continue**".
9. You can check and review Reekolect's access request and then click on save to move ahead.
10. After following the steps ahead your facebook bot will be ready to use.
11. You can now edit the flow for your bot. And you can now do customizations accordingly.



Your Facebook chatbot is created.

If everything appears correct but you are still unable to proceed, please don't hesitiate to call us. Our support team is here to assist you with any issues or questions you may have.
