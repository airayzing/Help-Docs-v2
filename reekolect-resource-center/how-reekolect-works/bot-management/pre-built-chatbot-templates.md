# 📖 Pre-Built Chatbot Templates

Reekolect offeres multiple templates that allow for our users to quickly and effeciently start their chatbot joirney.

### How to use a template?

1. Go to your chatbot and click on its name.
2. Click on the second option of "Pre-Built Chatbot Templates".
3. Search for the desired template using the left hand search bar, or select from the showcased templates by scrolling through the options.
   1. Users also have the option to filter their search via the filters under the search box on the left side of the scene.
4. For using a template, hover over the image and click the the "**Use Template**" icon.
5. Click "**Use Now**" to send the template to your flows, and be redirected to the chatflow, where you can start your editing!
