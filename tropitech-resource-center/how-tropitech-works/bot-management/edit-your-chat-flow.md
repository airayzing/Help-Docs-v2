# 📖 Edit Your Chat Flow

## **Overview**

A chat flow defines how conversations unfold between your chatbot and users. This guide will help you customize chat flows using the tools and features available on the Tropitech platform.

#### **Accessing the Chatbot Builder**

To customize your chatbot's flow, follow these steps:

1. **Go to the Tropitech Portal** – Navigate to the bot menu or homepage.
2. **Select Your Chatbot** – Click on the chatbot you want to modify.
3. **Open the Chatbot Builder** – Click on **"Edit Your Chat Flow"** to start customizing.

Now, you’re ready to structure your chatbot’s interactions to best suit your business needs!\

## **Adding & Customizing Components**

To add and customize components:

**1. Add New Component:**

* Either use the 'Add new component' tab or click the + icon at the end of the flow.
* Choose a component from the list to add it to the flow.

**2. Connect Components:**

Drag a line connector from one component to another to establish a connection.\\

**3. Personalize Messages:**

* Customize the message content and data to be shared from the options.
* Utilize 'Advanced Options' to adjust message timing and other settings.

## **Ending the Chat Flow**

To properly conclude the chat flow:

1. Navigate to the last component in the flow.
2. In the **"Go to next message"** dropdown, select **"End Chat"**.

Adding the **End Chat** component is essential to prevent system errors and ensure seamless chatbot functionality.
