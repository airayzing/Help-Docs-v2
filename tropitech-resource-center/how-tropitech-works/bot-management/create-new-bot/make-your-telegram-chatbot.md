# 📖 Make Your Telegram Chatbot

Follow these steps to **create and integrate** a Telegram bot using the **Tropitech platform.**

1. 1️⃣ **Log in** to the **Tropitech platform**.\
   2️⃣ Click on **"Create New Bot"** at the **top left** of the dashboard.\
   3️⃣ When prompted, **select "Telegram Chatbot"** as the platform.\
   4️⃣ Click **Proceed** to move to the next step.
2.  In the next step, you’ll be prompted to enter your **mobile number**. Make sure to provide the **Telegram number** under which you want to create the chatbot, then proceed with the setup.

    <figure><img src="../../../../.gitbook/assets/1 – 39.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
3.  1️⃣ Click **Next** after selecting the Telegram chatbot platform.\
    2️⃣ A **one-time verification code (OTP)** will be sent to your **Telegram number**.\
    3️⃣ Enter the **verification code** to confirm and verify your account.\


    <figure><img src="../../../../.gitbook/assets/1 – 40.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
4. After logging into Telegram, enter your bot name and a unique bot username that ends with “bot.” Once done, click **Proceed** to continue.
5. Your Telegram bot will be successfully created and ready to use. You can now edit and configure your chat flow, customize features, and optimize interactions as needed.

If you have any questions, feel free to reach out to our team at **soporte@tropitechnology.com**. We’ll get back to you within **48 business hours**.
